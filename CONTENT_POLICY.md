# Storyloom community rules

Use this as the initial community policy. The administrator must add their real contact information and adapt it to the countries where the service is available.

## What belongs here

- Original writing, comics, and stories you have permission to publish.
- Honest reviews and respectful discussion.
- Clear genre and audience tags. Suggested genres: `romance`, `thriller`, `comic`, `fantasy`, `literary`, `horror`, and `poetry`.

## What does not belong here

- Sexual content involving a minor, grooming, or material that sexualizes children.
- Credible threats, harassment, hate directed at protected groups, doxxing, or publication of private information.
- Copyright infringement, impersonation, scams, malware, or spam.
- Content illegal in the jurisdiction where the instance is run.

## Mature work

The free edition has no reliable automated content classifier. Writers must self-tag mature work with `mature`; moderators must review reports and may remove or reclassify material. Do not place mature work on the default reader timeline, youth-oriented tags, or public promotional pages.

For a first launch, the safest option is to accept only `everyone` and `teen` content. Add an adult-only space only after you have age-gating, an appeal process, and a moderator who can respond reliably.

## Reporting and enforcement

Readers should report concerns to the contact address shown on the Contact page. The administrator should preserve enough information to investigate, restrict access where necessary, document the decision, and offer a reasonable appeal path. Urgent child-safety reports require immediate attention and may require reporting to the competent authority.
