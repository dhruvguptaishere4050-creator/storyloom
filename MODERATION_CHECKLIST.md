# Free moderation checklist

## Before opening registration

- [ ] Put the real moderation email address on `/contact`.
- [ ] Review and publish `CONTENT_POLICY.md` as part of your community rules.
- [ ] Decide the launch audience: recommended first launch is 13+ and no mature-content section.
- [ ] Name at least one backup moderator.
- [ ] Make and test an offline backup of `storyloom.db` and `keys`.
- [ ] Create a private incident log that records report date, action, and reason without exposing personal data.

## Each new writer

- [ ] Invite manually; keep public registration disabled initially.
- [ ] Explain required genre and audience tags.
- [ ] Review first published posts before featuring them on the reader timeline.

## Each report

- [ ] Acknowledge receipt.
- [ ] Restrict clearly unsafe content while reviewing it.
- [ ] Record the decision and evidence needed to justify it.
- [ ] Tell the writer what rule was applied and how to appeal.
- [ ] Escalate child-safety issues according to applicable law and professional guidance.
