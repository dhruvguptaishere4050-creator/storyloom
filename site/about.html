About the project

A better home for original stories.

Storyloom is an open-source project exploring a thoughtful space for writers, comic creators, and readers. The aim is simple: authors keep control of their work, readers find stories through useful context, and community safety is designed in from the start.

What is live today

This public website, its source code, the content policy, and the project roadmap are available now. It is a way to follow the project and contribute to its development.

What is not open yet

Storyloom is not yet accepting accounts, story uploads, reviews, or public discussion. Those features will only open after secure hosting, backups, account deletion, in-app reporting and blocking, human moderation, and clear operational policies are ready.

Ownership

Creators stay creators.

Publishing tools should not take ownership away from the person who made the work.

Discovery

Context over tricks.

Genre, themes, content ratings, and reader shelves are more useful than opaque engagement algorithms.

Safety

Safety is not optional.

A community product needs clear rules, practical controls, and accountable human decisions.

Open source

Build in public.

The code is public so people can inspect it, improve it, and learn from it.

Want to contribute? Browse the project, open an issue, or propose an improvement on GitHub. Do not share private, personal, or urgent safety information in public issues.
