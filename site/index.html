Safety is a feature

Readers deserve an open shelf—not an unsafe one.

Storyloom’s public launch needs clear rules, reporting, user blocking, human review, and age-aware controls. Mature content must not be recommended to minors or appear in general discovery.

Read the content policy ↗
  • Clear community rules before posting
  • Report and block tools inside the app
  • Human action on urgent reports
  • No paid AI required for discovery
  • Privacy policy and account deletion before launch
How Storyloom grows
