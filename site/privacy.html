Privacy notice · Updated 12 September 2026

Privacy for the current website.

This notice applies only to the public Storyloom landing website at this address. It will be replaced and expanded before Storyloom launches accounts, story publishing, reviews, or any community features.

What this site collects

Storyloom does not currently provide account creation, uploads, comments, payments, newsletters, analytics, advertising, or a contact form. The project does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, story text, or other personal information through this landing site.

Hosting and basic technical data

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may process technical request information such as IP address, browser information, timestamps, and server logs to deliver and protect the website. GitHub’s handling of that information is governed by its own Privacy Statement ↗.

Local storage

The site uses a small service worker so recently visited files may be cached by your browser for faster loading. This cache stays on your device and can be cleared through your browser’s site-data controls. It does not send data to Storyloom.

External links

Links to GitHub and other third-party sites are governed by those sites’ own policies. Do not share sensitive information in a public GitHub issue.

Future service

Before the community platform opens, this notice will state exactly what account, content, moderation, security, and deletion data is collected; why it is used; who processes it; and how people can exercise their rights.

Questions or changes: Use the project’s contact page. A dedicated support and moderation email must be added before public publishing launches.
