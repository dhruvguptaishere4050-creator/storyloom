Safety first

A creative home must also be a safe one.

Storyloom will not open public posting just because the website looks ready. A writing community has a duty to protect readers, creators, and especially young people.

What the future platform must include

  • Clear community rules accepted before someone publishes or participates.
  • Separate, easy-to-find in-app tools to report content and block people.
  • Human review, timely action, records of important decisions, and an appeal path.
  • Age-aware content ratings and safe discovery defaults.
  • No recommendation of mature content in general or minor-facing discovery.
  • Secure account design, account deletion, backups, and a way to respond to abuse.

Content boundaries

Storyloom will not permit child sexual abuse material, credible threats, doxxing, harassment, hate, scams, malware, or copyright infringement. Mature themes require explicit labels and a proven age-protection and moderation process before they can be considered for any future service.

Current status

There is no public publishing, commenting, messaging, or reporting function yet. That is intentional. There is therefore nothing public to report on this landing site.

Urgent concerns

If there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a trusted local safeguarding organisation. Do not post sensitive personal information, evidence of harm, or emergency reports in a public GitHub issue.

Before public launch: the project operator must provide a real support and moderation email, staff the reporting process, publish a complete community policy, and test these controls with real users.
